# IRNIC 3-Character Domain Audit & Live Index

Comprehensive investigation, live WHOIS audit, and availability index of the 3-character `.ir` domain space conducted directly against the IRNIC registry and domestic registrar cart validation APIs.

## 🎯 Key Findings & Live Audit Statistics
- **3-Letter Pure Domains (`LLL.ir`):** **0 Free.** 100% of all 17,576 combinations (`aaa.ir` to `zzz.ir`) are registered.
- **3-Digit Pure Domains (`NNN.ir`):** **0 Free.** 100% of all 1,000 combinations (`000.ir` to `999.ir`) are registered.
- **Purchased After Introduction (🔥 ثبت‌شده توسط کاربران):** **57 Domains** from our initial curated recommendations were purchased and registered by visitors and community members.
- **Active Free Domains (دامنه‌های آزاد فعال):** **191 Verified Free Domains** in high-value developer, AI/ML, cloud, media, and publishing niches.
- **Total Monitored 3-Character Vault:** **267 Domains** continuously tracked.

## 🛠️ Automated Verification Architecture
Probes are executed with socket keep-alive connections against authoritative port 43 (`whois.nic.ir:43`) and SSL-unverified HTTPS endpoint pooling (`https://whois.nic.ir/WHOIS?name=<domain>`), cross-checked with domestic registrar cart checkout pipes.
